Doing good work

What makes
a task count.

There is no trick to it. Careful, honest work gets approved, and these four habits make the difference.

  1. 01

    Read the brief first

    Every task tells you what to do and what to send back. A minute spent reading saves you from doing it twice.

  2. 02

    Say what you really think

    Good or bad, honest feedback is what helps a product get better. Write it in your own words.

  3. 03

    Be specific

    "The checkout button was hard to find" helps far more than "it was okay". Small details are the useful part.

  4. 04

    Add proof when asked

    If a task asks for a screenshot or a link, include it. Complete work is easier to check and approve.

Privacy Policy

How we use your information

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Repuven is a website where members do review and survey tasks and earn money. This page explains what information we keep, why we keep it and what you can do about it. We wrote it in simple English so everyone can understand it.

01What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and the referral code you used to join.
  • Your password: it is handled by our login service. We cannot see your password.
  • Profile photo: only if you add one.
  • Payment details: the Binance Pay ID or bKash number you give us for withdrawals, and the transaction ID you enter for deposits.
  • Your work: the reviews, answers and screenshots you send for tasks, and tasks you post.
  • Messages: what you write in Support Chat, Live Chat and private chats.
  • Money records: your balance, earnings, deposits, withdrawals, wallet transfers and fees.
  • Technical data: your browser keeps you logged in, and our servers keep basic logs (for example the time of a request and your IP address) to keep the site safe.

02Why we use it

  • To create and run your account.
  • To check your tasks and pay you for approved work.
  • To add your deposits and send your withdrawals.
  • To give referral rewards to the member who invited you.
  • To stop fake accounts, cheating and fraud.
  • To answer you in Support Chat and send notices to your Mailbox.
  • To send emails about your account, like the password reset code.

03Who can see it

  • Our team: admins can see your account, work, payments and support messages, so they can check tasks and payments and help you.
  • Other members: they can see your name, photo and messages in Live Chat. They can see your name on the Leaderboard only if you turn it on.
  • Binance and bKash: when we send or receive money, they get the details needed for that payment.
  • Service providers: we use Supabase to store data, run logins and keep files, and an email service to send account emails. Their servers may be outside Bangladesh.

We do not sell your information to anyone.

04How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. We may keep money records for longer, because we need them for our accounts and to stop fraud. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise the rest.

05Your choices

  • Change your name and photo on the Profile page.
  • Turn the Leaderboard on or off at any time.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete your account. Send us a message in Support Chat.

06Keeping it safe

Each member can only see their own data. Admin pages are only for admin accounts. No website is 100% safe, so please keep your password secret. Our team will never ask for your password or the code we email you.

07Age

You must be 18 years or older to use Repuven.

08Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For big changes, we will also send a notice to your Mailbox.

09Contact us

Questions about your information? Send us a message in Support Chat. See the Help page.

Terms of Service

The rules for using Repuven

Last updated: 2 November 2026

When you create an account, you agree to these rules. Please read them before you start.

01Who can join

  • You must be 18 years or older.
  • Every account is joined with a referral code. If a member invited you, it is their code. If not, it is Repuven's code.
  • One person can have only one account. Use your real name and your own email.

02Your account

  • Keep your password secret. You are responsible for what happens in your account.
  • Do not sell, share or lend your account to anyone.
  • Tell us in Support Chat if you think someone else used your account.

03Tasks and reviews

  • Do every task yourself, and follow its steps.
  • Write only your honest opinion. Do not copy other people's reviews and do not write fake reviews.
  • If a task asks you to post on another website or app, follow that website's rules. Do not post there if its rules do not allow it.
  • Our team checks every task you send. We pay only for approved work. If your work is rejected, it is not paid.
  • How much you earn depends on the tasks that are open and on approved work. We do not promise any fixed income.

04Account verification

  • You need to verify your account to open the Survey page. Verification costs $20, one time. You pay it from your wallet balance.
  • After you verify, a 10% fee is taken from each withdrawal.
  • Accounts that verified earlier with the old $10 package keep their rules: a 50% fee until their total withdrawals reach $20.00, then 10%.
  • If you have a question, ask us in Support Chat before you pay.

05Deposits

  • Send money only to the Binance Pay ID or bKash number shown on the Deposit page at that time.
  • Then enter the amount and the correct transaction ID. We add the money to your wallet after we check it.
  • If a method says Closed, do not send money to it. Use the other method or try later.
  • Wrong or fake transaction IDs are rejected.

06Withdrawals

  • The minimum withdrawal is $1.00. There is no upper limit.
  • The fee is 10% of each withdrawal (see section 4).
  • We send the money to the Binance Pay ID or bKash number you enter. Check it carefully. If you enter a wrong number, we may not be able to get the money back.
  • Your request shows as Pending until our team checks it. It can take some time.

07Wallet transfers

You can send balance to another member with their wallet number. Check the number before you send. A transfer goes to the other member's wallet straight away.

08Referrals

  • Share your referral link only with real people.
  • You get a reward when a member you invited gets verified. Rewards are added to your wallet automatically.
  • Leadership levels, leader bonuses and the monthly Leader Pool follow the rules shown on your Profile. Rewards that come from fake accounts or self-invites are removed.
  • Inviting yourself or making fake accounts is not allowed.

09Chats

Be polite in Support Chat, Live Chat and private chats. No spam, no scams, no bad language and no sharing of other people's personal details. Admins can delete messages and block members from Live Chat.

10Cheating

If you cheat (for example with fake accounts, fake reviews, fake deposits or bots), we can reject your work, cancel the money you got by cheating, and suspend or close your account.

11Changes and service

  • We may change tasks, fees and these rules. We will send a notice to your Mailbox before a fee change applies to you.
  • We work hard to keep Repuven running, but sometimes it may be slow or closed for repairs.
  • We are not responsible for delays caused by Binance, bKash or your internet connection.

12Contact us

Questions about these rules? Send us a message in Support Chat. See the Help page.

Help & Support

How can we help?

Read the common questions below. If you still need help, send us a message in Support Chat. Our team replies there.

Support Chat

Log in, then open Support Chat from the menu. Only you and our team can see it.

Log in to chat

For your safety, never share your password or the code we email you. Our team will never ask for them.

I forgot my password

Click Log in, then Forgot password?. Enter your email. We will email you a code. Type the code on the page, then choose a new password. If you cannot find the email, check your Spam folder.

Do I need a referral code?

No. The code is filled in for you. If a member sent you their link, it is their code. If not, Repuven's code (ADMIN-SS-001) is used. If a friend gave you their code, you can type it instead.

How do I verify my account?

Log in and open your Profile page. Pay the one-time $20 verification from your wallet balance. After you are verified, the Survey page opens for you.

How long does a deposit take?

Send the money to the number shown on the Deposit page, then enter the amount and the transaction ID. Our team checks it and adds the money to your wallet. Make sure the transaction ID is correct. You can see the status in Your deposits.

How long does a withdrawal take?

Your request shows as Pending until our team checks it. When it is sent, the status changes to Approved. You can see it in Your withdrawals on the Withdraw page.

Why was my work rejected?

Usually because the steps were not followed, the proof was not clear, or the text was copied. Read the task steps again before you try another task.

A payment method says Closed

Do not send money to a closed method. Use the other method, or try again later.

How do I delete my account?

Send us a message in Support Chat. Withdraw your balance first, because a deleted account cannot be opened again.

How to add Google Authenticator
  1. Install the app. Get Google Authenticator from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). It's free.
  2. Tap Set up next to Google Authenticator above. We email you a code first, to make sure it's really you.
  3. Add Repuven to the app. In Google Authenticator tap + → Scan a QR code and point the camera at the code on your screen. On the same phone? Tap Open in Authenticator, or tap + → Enter a setup key and type the key we show you.
  4. Type the 6-digit code. The app shows Repuven with a code that changes every 30 seconds. Type the code you see now and tap Turn on.
  5. Done. When you withdraw or send money, choose Authenticator and type the code from the app.
